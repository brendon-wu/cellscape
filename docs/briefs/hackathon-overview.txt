Team Norms and

Part 1 Icebreakers and Personal Goals 5 minutes)

     Each member share:
           Their name
           One skill they bring to the hackathon
           A personal goal for the hackathon (e.g. learn
               something new, create a functioning prototype, etc!
           Favorite natural space :)
Part 2 Goal and Vision Setting 10 minutes)

     Define the shared goals for the team! Discuss:

           What does success look like for this hackathon?

           Does your team prioritize learning, winning, building
               something impactful, or something else?

     Write in your shared document:

           23 specific, measurable goals

                 "Complete a prototype of our project idea."
                 "Submit a pitch deck that clearly articulates our

                     solution."
                 "Ensure every team member contributes in a meaningful

                     way."
Part 3 Establish Norms and Roles 10 minutes)

    Discuss:

          Roles: Are there any role breakdowns? Who will lead coding,
              design, presentations, etc.?

          Communication: How will they communicate and keep everyone
              updated? (e.g., Slack, group texts).

          Time Management: When will they check in as a team, and how
              will they divide tasks?

          Conflict Resolution: What will they do if theres a disagreement?
    Write down:

          35 norms (e.g., "Listen to everyones ideas, "Decisions are
              made by majority vote).
Part 4 Share a Team Motto or Name 5 minutes)

     Create a team name that reflects your teams vision!
